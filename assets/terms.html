1. What Litterbox is

Litterbox is a free, non-commercial, fan-made platform where the Cat Goes Fishing community can share and discover mods. It's run by one person as a hobby project, not a business. Litterbox is an independent project and isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the creators of Cat Goes Fishing.

2. Who can use it

You need to be at least 13 years old to use Litterbox. If you're under 16, you need a parent or guardian's permission first, in line with EU and Polish law. By creating an account, you're confirming you meet this requirement.

3. Your account

  • Keep your login details to yourself - you're responsible for what happens under your account.
  • One account per person. Don't impersonate someone else or pretend to be a mod's creator when you're not.
  • We can suspend or remove an account that breaks these rules.

4. Your content

  • You keep ownership of the mods, descriptions, icons, banners, and posts you upload.
  • You need to actually own the rights to what you upload, or have clear permission to share it - don't upload someone else's mod as your own, or use assets you don't have rights to.
  • By uploading, you give Litterbox a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to host, store, display, and let others download that content through the site, for as long as it's on the site.
  • You can remove your own mods at any time from the Manage page.

5. Rules

Don't upload or post anything that:

  • Contains malware, viruses, or other harmful code
  • Isn't actually yours to share, or that you copied from someone without permission
  • Is illegal, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, or endangers or exploits minors in any way
  • Is spam or unrelated to Cat Goes Fishing modding

Also don't try to abuse, exploit, or circumvent the site's systems - including its moderation, security, or rate limits. If you sign in with Discord, Discord's own rules apply to that as well.

6. Moderation

Mods uploaded with a file go through a quick manual review before they appear publicly. Preview-only mods (uploaded without a file yet) are visible immediately, clearly labeled "Preview." We can reject, remove, or take down any content - and suspend or ban accounts - at our discretion, especially for breaking the rules above.

Anyone can report a mod that seems to break the rules, and we review every report. If your mod is rejected, you'll see why on its Manage page - you're welcome to fix the issue and reach out if you think it was a mistake.

Mods are made and uploaded by other community members, not by Litterbox itself. We do a manual review before a mod goes public, but we can't guarantee every file is completely safe or bug-free. Download and run mods at your own judgment - and if something seems off, please report it.
