Welcome to Catnip

Catnip swaps data.win files to switch between mods for Cat Goes Fishing. Your original (vanilla) file gets backed up automatically the first time, before any mod ever touches it.

Before you start: switch to the Gamma beta

Modding only works on Cat Goes Fishing's Gamma beta branch. In Steam:

  1. Right-click Cat Goes Fishing in your library → Properties
  2. Open the Betas tab
  3. Pick gamma from the dropdown and let it update
